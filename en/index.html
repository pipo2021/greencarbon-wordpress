Green Carbon BoliviaOrigin and purpose

Climate action from the territory.

We are a Bolivian company specialized in the territorial implementation of nature-based climate solutions, with experience in forest conservation, indigenous governance, environmental monitoring and community development.

We work where conservation really happens: in the territory.
